# Northwind Engineering — Company Profile

> Fictional employer for the HR-compliance assistant demo. All names, sites, and figures are invented. Structured machine-readable version lives in `data/sites.json`; this file is the human-readable backdrop that makes the personas and compliance scenarios hang together.

## Overview
**Northwind Engineering** is a mid-size **civil & environmental engineering consultancy** headquartered in Austin, Texas. Founded in 2009, the firm delivers infrastructure design, environmental assessment, geotechnical studies, and field survey services to public and private clients across the southern and western United States. As of 2026 it employs roughly **300 people** across three worksites in two states.

Because Northwind has **300+ employees company-wide**, it is a **covered employer** under the FMLA — but, as the demo shows, coverage at the company level does not guarantee eligibility at every worksite.

## What they do
- **Civil engineering** — roadway, water/wastewater, and site-development design.
- **Environmental** — impact assessments, permitting, remediation oversight.
- **Geotechnical & field services** — soil investigation, survey crews, on-site construction observation.

## Workforce composition
Northwind's staff split cleanly into the two FLSA categories, which is why pay-compliance questions come up daily:
- **Exempt (salaried):** licensed engineers, project managers, senior consultants. Not entitled to overtime.
- **Non-exempt (hourly):** field technicians, survey-crew members, drafters/CAD techs, and administrative/support staff. Entitled to overtime; subject to minimum-wage rules that differ between the California and Texas sites.

The mix of **two states** (CA vs. TX) and **two pay classifications** (exempt vs. non-exempt) is what generates the firm's real-world compliance questions.

## Why an HR-compliance assistant
Northwind's small HR team administers leave and pay across both jurisdictions. A single HR admin must, for any given employee, correctly apply:
- **FMLA** — is this employee eligible for job-protected leave, and how much?
- **FLSA** — is their pay at or above the applicable minimum, and is overtime owed (federal weekly, plus California's daily rules)?
- **Benefits** — plan-level questions (401k match/vesting, insurance, whether coverage continues during FMLA leave).

The assistant helps the HR admin process these per-employee, grounds every answer in company policy and the governing law with citations, flags where the handbook conflicts with the legal floor, and routes each drafted determination through the HR admin for approval before it is finalized.
